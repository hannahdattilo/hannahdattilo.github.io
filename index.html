Welcome!

My name is Hannah Dattilo and I am a fourth year PhD Candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Vanderbilt University working with Dr. Robert Reed. I received my B.S. in Physics from Oglethorpe University in Atlanta, GA in 2020 and my M.S. in Physics from Fisk University in Nashville, TN through the Fisk-Vanderbilt Master's-to-PhD Bridge program in 2022.

I am interested in the reliability of space-based electronic and photonic systems and ensuring radiation hardness of those systems. My PhD work relvolves around the effects of radiation on oxides and how those effects impact photonic device operation. Additionally, I care deeply about science communication at all knowledge levels: general public to higher ed to industy scientists and engineers. I love to integrate teaching science into my work and life as frequently as possible. If interested, you can click on the tabs above or links below to learn more about my research and sci-com efforts.

My freetime is dedicated to my many hobbies. I love the fiber arts, including crochet, tapestry weaving, and fabric dyeing, as well as pottery. I also like to climb (indoors and outdoors) and play tennis, pickleball, or any racquet sport. And, i have three cats that keep me busy. I am also in a local horror bookclub, Ghosts and Roasts. Scroll for more info about my art projects, outdoor adventures, and kitties.
